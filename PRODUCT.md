# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Laravel JSON API with dependency-free vanilla ES-module SPA.

## Users

University voters casting ballots on mobile devices and election administrators preparing and monitoring elections on desktop.

## Product Purpose

UniVote EVS lets a university publish elections, collect one ballot per voter per position, and review trustworthy results.

## Positioning

Election setup, ballot structure, lifecycle, voting, auditability, and results live in one first-party system.

## Operating Context

Admins prepare scheduled elections in advance; voters review candidates and cast ballots during a configured voting window.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Laravel 13 API, Sanctum authentication, PostgreSQL, and a vanilla ES-module SPA styled with Tailwind CSS. Existing vote uniqueness and audit logging remain authoritative.

## Brand Commitments

Product name: UniVote EVS. University identity is institutional, with replaceable university name and crest slots.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing election, position, candidate, user, vote, results, and audit API plus SPA views.

## Product Principles

- Make election state obvious.
- Keep ballot setup close to the election it serves.
- Make voting calm and reversible until final submission.
- Preserve auditability and server-side enforcement.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation, visible focus, mobile readability, semantic controls, and reduced-motion support are required.
