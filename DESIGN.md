# UniVote EVS Design

## World

University registrar materials translated into a precise digital workspace: cool paper surfaces, ink typography, campus green actions, oxblood warnings, and brass confirmation accents.

## Tokens

- Ink: `#18211E`
- Campus green: `#0F5B46`
- Oxblood: `#8D2638`
- Brass: `#B78324`
- Paper: `#F7FAF8`
- Mist: `#DCE8E2`

## Type

Source Serif 4 for page titles and election names; Public Sans for controls and body copy; monospace only for timestamps, IDs, and measurements.

## Composition

Voters use a quiet top navigation and mobile-first ballot. Admins use a persistent workspace rail and an election-scoped control surface with explicit lifecycle stages.

## Signature

The election docket: every admin election view carries a compact status rail showing preparation, scheduled window, live voting, and results readiness.
